Sign in your browser. Carry it anywhere.

Your key is made and kept in this page by the browser's own cryptography. Nothing is uploaded: a signed transaction is a small file you hand to any validator, from any machine, even one that has never seen your key. That separation — signing offline, submitting online — is how high-value systems keep keys away from networks.
