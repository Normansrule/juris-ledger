The economy and the network, block by block.

What a statistics office or a network operator watches: how much is happening, where the money goes, how gross domestic product (GDP) builds up, and whether the validators are keeping up. Fed by jurisledger api, by a metrics.csv, or by the bundled sample.

not connected

To watch a real network: jurisledger index 127.0.0.1:7701 -o ledger.db, then jurisledger api ledger.db --follow 127.0.0.1:7701, then press Connect live. The page refreshes every five seconds.

Consensus health

Validator signatures on each block (bars) and extra voting rounds needed (dots). Rounds above zero mean a proposer was slow or the network was delayed.
