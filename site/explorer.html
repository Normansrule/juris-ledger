      Follow one account

      This page checks the ledger's structure and every signature against the validators named in its founding record. It does not re-run balances, nonces and contract rules; for the complete check run jurisledger audit chain.json. A ledger whose validator set changed by governance vote may show certificate warnings here that the full audit accepts.
