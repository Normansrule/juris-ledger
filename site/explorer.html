      Review flags

      The four detectors of jurisledger fraud, run here on the ledger you opened: money moving in a circle, payments just under a reporting threshold, amounts whose first digits look invented, and one invoice financed by several lenders. A flag is a lead for a person, not a finding — ordinary trade triggers some. Click one to light up its blocks.

      This page checks the ledger's structure and every signature against the validators named in its founding record. It does not re-run balances, nonces and contract rules; for the complete check run jurisledger audit chain.json. A ledger whose validator set changed by governance vote may show certificate warnings here that the full audit accepts.
